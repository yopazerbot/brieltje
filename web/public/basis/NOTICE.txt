basis_transcoder.js and basis_transcoder.wasm in this folder are the Basis Universal transcoder as
shipped with three.js (three/examples/jsm/libs/basis/, three 0.186.1). basis_transcoder.wasm is
unchanged. basis_transcoder.js is changed: two functions of its bindings no longer run generated
source text through new Function() (the change is described at the top of the file). Licence:
Apache 2.0 (LICENSE.txt in this folder). The attribution notice of Basis Universal follows.
