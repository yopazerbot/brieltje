1. Who runs this

Brieltje (brieltje.be) is a free online game: a walkable 3D model of a house in Briel (Lede, Belgium) and its surroundings. It is run and paid for by Yoshi Parlevliet CommV, a Belgian partnership (commanditaire vennootschap), enterprise number 1002.569.036, registered office: Lede. Contact: yoshi@olive-security.be.

2. Using the game

The game is free. By playing it you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

brieltje.be is only open to visitors in Belgium. Visits from other countries are blocked, based on the country of your IP address, to keep abuse and attacks out. Want to play from somewhere else? Email yoshi@olive-security.be and we'll see what we can do. Getting around the block, for example with a VPN, isn't allowed.

3. Play fair

Not allowed:

  • cheating, using bugs, or changing the game in your browser to gain an advantage;
  • attacking, overloading or probing the server: denial of service, scanning, guessing PINs or passwords, bots or scrapers;
  • trying to reach the admin functions, other players' sessions, or data that isn't yours;
  • offensive, hateful, discriminating, threatening, sexual or illegal names or chat messages, and harassing other players;
  • pretending to be someone else, or sharing other people's personal details;
  • using the game to find, watch or bother the people living in the real houses it shows. They are private homes: respect the people who live there.

4. Moderation

To keep the game pleasant and the server running, the admin can remove players, refuse names and block addresses, with or without warning. Serious abuse can be reported to the hosting provider or the police.

5. Multiplayer

Playing together needs the PIN the admin gives out. The chat isn't private: everyone in the game, and the admin, can read it. Don't share personal details in it.

6. No guarantees

The game is offered as it is, without any warranty. It can change, lose saved things (the leaderboard, the furniture layout) or go offline at any time.

7. Liability

As far as Belgian law allows, Yoshi Parlevliet CommV is not liable for any damage from using the game or from not being able to use it. This does not limit liability for intent or gross negligence, or any liability the law doesn't allow to be limited.

8. Health

The game has flashing effects (explosions, portal flashes). If you are sensitive to flashing light, take care. Take a break now and then.

Found a security problem? Report it to yoshi@olive-security.be. Please don't test it against other players or in a way that slows the game down.
