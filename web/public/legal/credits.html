Our own work

The house, the church, the school, the bungalow, the cars, the animals, the furniture, the zombies of the Zombies game and everything else modelled in Blender by the scripts in the repository. The zombies are original characters made for the game. The facade brick, the neighbours' brick and roof tiles and the church square's clinkers are generated.

Textures and sky

Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain): plaster, tiles, floors, concrete, pavers, gravel, fields, wood, leaves, the brick and roof tiles of the houses round the church square, and the skies: the sunny, the overcast, the dusk and the night sky.

ambientCG, CC0 (public domain): the grass of lawns, meadows and verges.

Characters

Quaternius, CC0, via Poly Pizza: the SWAT character and the cat.

Map data

© Digitaal Vlaanderen, under the Modellicentie Gratis Hergebruik: the GRB (buildings, plots, roads), DHM Vlaanderen II (the laser scan of heights), the orthophoto and the road register (street names).

What grows on the fields: the Landbouwgebruikspercelen (the crop declared on each parcel), under the Modellicentie Gratis Hergebruik. Bron: Departement Landbouw en Visserij. Only the crop's name is used.

Where the traffic signs stand, which way they face and what they show: Verkeersborden.Vlaanderen, under the Modellicentie Gratis Hergebruik. Bron: MOW - AWV (Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer).

Where the bus stops are, and where FC Smetlede's football ground is: © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License (ODbL).
