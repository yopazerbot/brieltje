Our own work

The house, the church, the school, the bungalow, the cars, the animals, the furniture and everything else modelled in Blender by the scripts in the repository. The facade brick and the neighbours' brick and roof tiles are generated.

Textures and sky

Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain): plaster, tiles, floors, concrete, pavers, gravel, fields, grass, wood, leaves, the brick and roof tiles of the houses round the church square, and the sky.

Characters

Quaternius, CC0, via Poly Pizza: the SWAT character, the pistol, rifle, shotgun and sniper rifle, and the cat.

Map data

© Digitaal Vlaanderen, under the Modellicentie Gratis Hergebruik: the GRB (buildings, plots, roads), DHM Vlaanderen II (the laser scan of heights), the orthophoto and the road register (street names).

Software

three.js, ws and node-postgres (MIT), Vite and TypeScript, Octicons for the GitHub mark, and Blender, Shapely and PyMuPDF for the tools.

Reference only
