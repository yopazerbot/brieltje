## Facade Brick

Generated by `tools/textures.py` to match the house's light beige blended facing brick and its brick size. The neighbours' brick (standard format) and roof tiles are generated there too, and tinted per house in the game. So is the asphalt of Molenhoek's rebuilt street, tinted dark grey for the road and red for the cycle lanes.

## Effects and Horizon

Drawn in code at start-up, no image files: smoke, fire, sparks and debris sprites, muzzle flashes and bullet holes (`web/src/world/sprites.ts`), the garden fence wire, and the tree lines on the horizon.

## Our Own Models

Built in Blender by the scripts in `tools/` (our own work, no third-party assets, no logos or lettering): the Tesla Model Y and the blue Model 3 (`model_y.py`, `model_3.py`), the twenty-seven houses round Smetlede's church square, along the street to the school and on Kerkhofstraat, the café by the church tower among them (`town/town.py`, from the survey in `town/houses.json`; the café's name on its sign board is lettered in DejaVu Sans, Blender's built-in font, under the Bitstream Vera licence), the office chair, coffee machine, soundbar and toilets (`aeron.py`, `coffee_machine.py`, `soundbar.py`, `toilet.py`), the bungalow's arched fanlight (`fanlight.py`), the horses (`horse.py`), the forty-seven houses along Molenhoek from the church square to the edge of the map and the street in front of them (`molenhoek.py`, on the open data's plans and heights, their looks from a Street View survey used only as reference), the forty-nine houses along Kapellenhoek from the church square to past its junction with Briel and on the first stretch of Kapelleweg (the same builder, from `kapellenhoek_survey.py`, the same way), the clown (`clown.py`), an original character of our own, and the strongman (`strongman.py`). The strongman has the face of a friend of Yoshi's, who asked to be in the game; it is a crop of a photo he sent, inside `strongman.glb` (so it can be downloaded from the site), and the photo itself stays out of git (`source/people/`).

## Houses Round the Portals at Home

The neighbouring houses round the house portal and the bungalow portal, the bungalow's roof trim and its front terrace are built in Blender by `tools/homestreet/houses.py` (our own work, no third-party assets), after the GRB outlines, the laser scan's heights, the orthophoto and our own notes from Street View (reference only, nothing of Google's in the game). Their brick, roof tile and timber textures (`web/public/textures/hs_*.webp`) are generated by `tools/homestreet/textures.py`; the render and paving use the Poly Haven textures above.

## Garden

Our own layout (`data/garden.json`): paths, terrace, driveway, planted borders, the wadi and the covers in the ground.
